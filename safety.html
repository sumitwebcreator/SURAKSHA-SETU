Simple personal safety habits

  • Keep emergency numbers and a trusted contact easily accessible.
  • Do not share OTPs, passwords or banking PINs with anyone.
  • Verify unknown calls, links and requests for money.
  • Tell a trusted person before travelling alone when appropriate.
  • In immediate danger, contact official emergency services.
