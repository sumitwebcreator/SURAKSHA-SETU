About Suraksha Setu

An India-focused safety and support platform concept built around care, connection and accessibility.

A bridge between people and help.

Suraksha Setu is designed with senior citizens in mind, especially people who may live alone or need easy access to emergency information. Its features can also support women, men, families and caregivers during unexpected situations.

The main idea is to bring useful services into one organised place: emergency helplines, trusted family contacts, consent-based information sharing, daily care planning and accessible safety guidance.

Our design principles

  • Simple navigation and readable content.
  • Respect for user privacy and consent.
  • Support for senior citizens and people of all ages.
  • Useful information presented in one place.
