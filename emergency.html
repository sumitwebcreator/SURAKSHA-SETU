Emergency Services

Important helpline numbers in India. In immediate danger, contact the appropriate official service.

Numbers are general public helplines. Availability and coverage may vary. This website does not dispatch emergency teams.

National Emergency

112

Pan-India emergency response number.

Call 112

Ambulance

108

Emergency medical ambulance service in many states.

Call 108

Police

112

Use the national emergency number for urgent police help.

Call 112

Fire & Rescue

112

Use the national emergency number for urgent fire and rescue support.

Call 112

Women Helpline

181

Women helpline; availability may differ by state.

Call 181

Senior Citizens Helpline

14567

Elderline, national helpline for senior citizens.

Call 14567

Child Helpline

1098

Child helpline for children in need of care and protection.

Call 1098

Cyber Crime

1930

Report financial cyber fraud promptly.

Call 1930

Before you call

  • Stay as calm as possible and explain the situation clearly.
  • Share your location and a nearby landmark.
  • Follow instructions from the official emergency operator.
  • Keep a trusted family member informed when safe.
